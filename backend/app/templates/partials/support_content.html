Account

Use the password reset link on the sign-in page. If you cannot verify your account or your staff details are incorrect, contact HR to check your registered staff ID and work email.
Appraisals

Contact HR with the appraisal cycle and the employee or supervisor names. HR can check the cycle and assignment records.
Appraisals

Submitted appraisals are read-only while they are under review. If a correction is needed, ask your supervisor or HR about returning the appraisal for changes.
Troubleshooting

Check your internet connection, then refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, contact HR and include the page name, what you were doing, and any error message shown.
