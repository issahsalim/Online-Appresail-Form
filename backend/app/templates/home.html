How the appraisal works

Each stage builds on the last, with clear responsibilities and constructive review.

  1. 01
    Your appraisal begins

    Employee

    Set work goals, record training and choose relevant competencies.

  2. 02
    Guidance and feedback

    Supervisor

    Review progress, score selected competencies and share feedback.

  3. 03
    Department review

    Head of Department

    Add a departmental perspective and support a fair review.

  4. 04
    Final approval

    Human Resources

    Complete the final review and confirm the appraisal outcome.
