Appraisal Roles

Employee Supervisor Head of Department Human Resources Support

Employee Appraisal

The appraisal starts with your reflection on the work period. Record clear targets, relevant learning, and progress so your reviewer has a useful picture of your contribution.

PHASE 01

Prepare your appraisal

Sign in to the staff portal and complete your personal information, work goals, training, and selected competencies.

Before you submit

  • Write specific targets that connect to your responsibilities.
  • Describe the resources or support needed to meet each target.
  • Add an overall reflection on your work and development needs.
PHASE 02

Submit for review

Review your entries carefully, save your work, and submit the appraisal to your assigned supervisor.

  • Keep your submission reference number for follow-up.
  • Use the appraisal history area to view progress or print a submitted copy.
PHASE 03

Discuss progress

Meet with your supervisor to discuss achievements, challenges, and practical next steps for your growth.

PHASE 04

Follow the outcome

Track the review as it moves through the supervisor, department head, and HR stages. If it is returned to you, update the requested items and resubmit.

Supervisor Review

Supervisors guide staff through a fair review of their targets, learning, and performance during the appraisal period.

PHASE 01

Review the submission

Open assigned appraisals from your dashboard and check that the employee’s goals and supporting comments are clear.

  • Discuss progress and clarify any incomplete information.
  • Return the appraisal with specific guidance when updates are needed.
PHASE 02

Provide feedback

Record constructive comments and complete the review fields assigned to the supervisor stage.

PHASE 03

Submit to the department

Once the review is complete, submit the appraisal to the Head of Department and monitor its status.

PHASE 04

Support next steps

Follow up on agreed development actions and help the employee prepare for the next review period.

Department Review

The Head of Department adds a department-level perspective and confirms that the appraisal reflects the employee’s work and responsibilities.

PHASE 01

Check the review record

Review the employee and supervisor entries, comments, targets, and progress evidence.

PHASE 02

Add department feedback

Record a balanced departmental assessment and identify any support or development that should be considered.

PHASE 03

Return or forward

If clarification is needed, return the appraisal with a clear reason. Otherwise, submit it to Human Resources.

PHASE 04

Confirm completion

Track the appraisal through final HR review and communicate any agreed follow-up to the employee.

Human Resources

HR manages appraisal cycles, staff assignments, and final review so records are complete and available for follow-up.

PHASE 01

Set up the cycle

Configure the appraisal cycle and confirm employee, supervisor, and department assignments.

PHASE 02

Monitor submissions

Track appraisals waiting for review and support staff who need help completing the process.

PHASE 03

Complete final review

Check the submitted record, complete the HR decision, and approve or return it with clear feedback.

PHASE 04

Maintain records

Keep completed appraisals and cycle records available to authorized staff for reporting and future planning.

Important notes for staff

  • Use your own staff account and keep your sign-in details private.
  • Write accurate, specific information that relates to your role and review period.
  • Discuss goals and feedback with your supervisor throughout the cycle.
  • Keep the submission reference number for questions about your appraisal.
  • Contact HR or Support if your reviewer or cycle information appears incorrect.
